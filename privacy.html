Privacy Policy

AppleVault Rclone is a private application used solely by the owner to back up the owner's Google Drive data to privately controlled local storage.

The application uses read-only access to Google Drive. It does not modify or delete files in Google Drive.

Data accessed through Google APIs is not sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties. Data is copied only to storage controlled by the owner.

AppleVault Rclone is not a public service and does not provide accounts or access to other users.
